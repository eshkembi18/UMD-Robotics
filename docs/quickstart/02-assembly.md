## Safety

!!! warning
    Keep servos unpowered during the whole assembly. Don't force servo horns onto their splines, because that can damage the gears.

!!! danger "Servo IDs first?"
    Depending on the guide you follow, servos may need IDs assigned **before** they go into the arm, since some are hard to reach once mounted. Read [step 3](03-servo-ids.md) before closing up the arm. `[TEAM VERIFICATION REQUIRED: write down the order we actually used]`
